# Subagent Briefs

Every delegated unit must receive a complete, bounded brief:

```text
GOAL
SCOPE
CONTEXT
ACCEPTANCE
VERIFY
TIMEBOX
FORBIDDEN
REPORT
STANDING ORDERS
```

Do not delegate when a required field is unknown. The brief must identify
writeable paths, forbidden paths, dependencies, exact checks, escalation rules,
and the expected report shape. TARS owns the brief and integrates the result.
